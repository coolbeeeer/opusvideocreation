## Accuracy notes
- Penicillin and related drugs block the enzymes that cross-link the bacterial cell wall (peptidoglycan).
  The weakened wall gives way and the cell bursts. Human cells have no cell wall, so they are not affected.
- "Protein factories" are bacterial ribosomes, which differ from human ones (targeted by tetracyclines and macrolides).
  "Copying its DNA" refers to drugs such as fluoroquinolones, which block the enzymes that copy bacterial DNA.
- Antibiotics have no effect on viruses.
- Resistance: bacteria that already resist a drug survive treatment and multiply, and many can pass resistance genes to other bacteria.
- The closing advice follows the current wording "exactly as prescribed" rather than the older "always finish the course".
